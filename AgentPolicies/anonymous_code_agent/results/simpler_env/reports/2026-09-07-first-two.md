# First two visual code-agent development attempts

Status: both episodes closed and independently reviewed. This is a small development
sample, not a full benchmark score or a verified provider/API evaluation.

Both tasks were selected before execution, with one scored attempt each, a
predeclared `extended-2x` track, at most 40 policy decisions, and at most five
executed actions per decision. Each episode uses a fresh actor context. Actors
receive only mailbox RGB/language/action specifications and return action JSON.
Provider requests, tokens and cost are unavailable and recorded as `null`.

| Task | Native H | Actual limit | Executed steps | Decisions (including stop) | Native result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Google horizontal Coke can | 80 | 160 | 121 | 33 | success; policy stopped |
| WidowX spoon on towel | 60 | 120 | 120 | 38 | failure; environment truncated |

The Google trajectory's native success at step 80 was **false**; final native
success at step 121 was **true**. Step 160 was not reached, so its checkpoint is
`null`. This is an extended-track success, not an 80-step success or an independent
native-budget run. No scored-attempt restart, invalid-response repair, or task-level
retry was used. Upstream environment construction itself calls reset internally;
this does not claim exactly one native reset invocation was instrumented.

First native success occurred at **step 113, during policy call 31**. Logged steps
113 through 121 were all successful (nine consecutive steps). The actor then
submitted stop at decision 33 based on consecutive visual lift observations,
without receiving native success feedback. First native success, the actor's
visual judgment, and final scoring are distinct measurements.

The actor reported a visible lift and submitted stop before seeing the score.
The closed native result separately records grasped=true, consec_grasp=true and
n_lift_significant=8. Those private metrics were not supplied to either actor.

Google used 32 action-bearing decisions plus one stop, hence 33 decisions but
121 control steps. One response can execute one to five actions; a stop executes
none. First success is reported as both environment step **113** and decision
**31**, not as a substitute for final native scoring. Subsequent decision-primary
protocols resolve a D×K safety cap before reset; they do not extend these attempts.

Known limitations: the mailbox is not an OS sandbox; the underlying model
snapshot and actual provider request counts are not verified. The first run's
manifest predates a committed harness/source snapshot. A later provenance fix
must not rewrite its historical manifest or imply retrospective reproducibility.
Both historical endpoints used wire v1 absolute paths containing `20260907`,
which was also the reset seed. This is an identifier-blinding gap even without
evidence that actors interpreted the identifier as a seed or used an oracle.
The subsequent v2 relative-path/random-endpoint fix cannot retroactively remove it.
